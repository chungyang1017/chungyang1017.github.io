第一節

為什麼是兒童

Why children? New risks and social inheritance

這一節要回答　社會在老化，為什麼反而要多投資兒童？

本節文獻　★ Esping-Andersen (2002: 26–49)

Esping-Andersen, G. (2002). A child-centred social investment strategy. In G. Esping-Andersen, D. Gallie, A. Hemerijck & J. Myles, Why We Need a New Welfare State (pp. 26–67). Oxford University Press.

本章是全書最長、資料最密的一章。表 2.1 到 2.10 共十張表，多數用 LIS（盧森堡所得研究）與 ECHP（歐洲共同體家戶追蹤調查）資料，年份在 1980 年代到 1990 年代中期。

這一節講前半：家戶的新風險結構、單親與無工作家戶、讓工作有代價與活化。02 節講後半的預防策略。

講解

原文 (p. 26)：Europe was a youthful continent in the post-war decades and, yet, its social welfare policies came to focus very much on the elderly. Now that our societies are ageing it is becoming quite urgent that we invest far more in the welfare of children. This would all appear a bit paradoxical.

作者的解消 (p. 26)：福利資本主義同時提供了所得安全，以及傳統家庭內充裕的照顧能力。加上婚姻穩定，社會政策可以假定兒童與家庭不是最急迫的議題。

老人則不同。強制退休擴散、三代同堂式微，而戰後退休的那一代是歷史上不幸的一代：一次大戰時年輕，成年職涯橫跨大蕭條與二次大戰，累積的資源很少。

這一段可以接回第 4 週的出生世代概念：福利國家的優先順序，本身反映了某些出生世代的歷史遭遇。

講解

原文 (pp. 26–27)：In the new economy, the fundamental requisites for a good life are rising ever more. This implies that life chances depend increasingly on the cultural, social, and cognitive capital that citizens can amass. The fundamental life phase is in childhood, and the crucial issue lies in the inter-play between parental and societal investments in children's development. The new paradox, then, is that strong families are becoming ever more detrimental for our well-being even as they are themselves becoming more vulnerable, fragile, and threatened.

原文的 detrimental 照字面是「有害的」，與上下文不合。依文意應是 determinant（決定性的），這裡照文意譯為「決定」。

p. 27：It is, however, from a life course perspective that we can identify the real importance of investing in the well-being and resources of children. 這一句就是上週 Leisering (2003: 222) 說本書以生命歷程為參照軸的依據之一。

講解

表 2.1 (p. 28)：依變項是文件識字分數，自變項是父親的教育程度。表題寫 Parents' education，表註寫 fathers' level of educational attainment，以表註為準。

作者的讀法 (p. 27)：父母效果仍強。跨國差異似乎和整體社會不平等有關：在斯堪地那維亞系統地較弱，在美國強得多。作者的基本預設是：北歐較低的繼承效果，來自這些國家對兒童與家庭的非凡投資。

讀表提醒：兩欄是不同的出生世代。瑞典 16–25 歲 0.23、25–65 歲 0.39，年輕世代較低，符合「繼承在下降」的說法。德國正好相反，年輕世代 0.27 高於年長世代 0.17。作者在 pp. 55–56 也說德國、比利時與美國的父母影響很強而且在增加。

註 19 (p. 27)：教育與職業成就仍和過去一樣分層（Erikson & Goldthorpe 1992；Shavit & Blossfeld 1992）。唯一的例外是瑞典，繼承效果確實下降。

講解

原文 (pp. 28–29)：The first would result from an inegalitarian trajectory, nurturing 'islands of excellence in a sea of ignorance'. The United States may be heading in this direction when, on one hand, we consider its world dominance of centres of scientific excellence and, on the other hand, the shocking fact that about 20 per cent of adult Americans fall into the bottom, essentially dysfunctional, category of cognitive abilities. The second, more egalitarian route, would look more like a 'tranquil pond with few ripples'. The Nordic countries (with no Harvard University but with less than 5 per cent in the dysfunctional category) appear closer to this scenario.

p. 29：There may very well be a kind of Keynsian limit to the knowledge economy that will be reached when a large citizenry lacks the abilities to consume knowledge products. 原文拼作 Keynsian。

這一頁說明作者的論證有兩條腿。p. 27 是公平：生命機會仍由社會繼承決定。p. 28 是效率：未來的工作世代人數少，卻要撐起龐大的退休人口，唯一真正的資產是人的品質與技能。

講解

p. 29：The mainsprings of people's life chances lie in the family conditions of their childhood. And, once adult, it is the household that gives meaning to the unfolding individual life course. It is here that social advantage and disadvantage are transmitted and activated; it is here that social risks and needs find primary expression; and it is also here that the primary social safety net is found.

p. 30：Probably all would concur that social exclusion occurs when citizens are trapped in inferior life chances. If this indeed is the core issue, then it is essential that we focus our lens on childhood and family welfare ... Remedial policies once people have reached adulthood are unlikely to be effective unless these adults started out with sufficient cognitive and social skills. A social investment strategy directed at children must be a centrepiece of any policy for social inclusion.

p. 32：marriage behaviour remains highly homogamous. The weak bundle together with the weak, the strong with the strong.

社會排除一詞原由法國提出，原本指被排除在主流社會保險之外的人 (pp. 29–30)。作者把它擴大成生命機會的問題。

講解

p. 32：The social insurance model is also inadequate in meeting the new risk structure because, almost by definition, it secures those who already enjoy security—the stably employed—while excluding those at the fringes. It deepens, in other words, the divide between insiders and outsiders.

p. 32：In Italy, among the unemployed 20–30 year-olds, 90 per cent depend totally on parental support. 作者說這種家庭解方有延後獨立、成家與就業的副作用。這一點下週（教育與青年轉銜）會再出現。

p. 33：nations that favour targeting, like the British and American, perform quite poorly in terms of offsetting new inequalities.

這一頁也可以接第 10 週的二元化。Esping-Andersen 在 2002 年已經用 insiders 與 outsiders 描述社會保險的效果。

講解

表 2.3 (p. 34) 的「有子女家庭」欄與趨勢欄。各國年份不同：丹麥 1987/97、芬蘭 1987/95、瑞典 1987/95、荷蘭 1987/94、法國 1984/94、德國（西）1984/94、義大利 1986/95、英國 1986/95、美國 1986/97。

趨勢（百分點）：丹麥 +2.6、芬蘭 +1.2、瑞典 −1.3、荷蘭 +2.4、法國 −0.4、德國（西）+10.0、義大利 +7.4、英國 +3.8、美國 +2.5。

原文 (p. 34)：When we examine all households, poverty has remained fairly stable (with Germany being a main exception) in most countries over the 1980s–1990s. But this disguises two opposite trends. Poverty has fallen significantly among the elderly while rising among families with children.

貧窮持續 (p. 35)：以 1993–6 年每年都貧窮為定義，希臘、義大利、西班牙、英國有 4–6% 的有子女家庭連續四年貧窮；丹麥 0.1%，法國 1.7%。美國總貧窮率略高於丹麥的三倍，持續率卻是丹麥的十倍。

講解

母親就業是最具決定性的單一因素 (p. 36)。

表 2.4 (p. 37) 節錄 6 國。原表另有比利時（11、23）、荷蘭（17、41）、西班牙 1990（有工作 24，移轉 −20.6）、丹麥（10、34，移轉欄印「—」）、芬蘭（有工作 5，移轉 −38.2）。

法國與丹麥，母親就業使貧窮勝算降低將近 8 倍 (p. 36)。

原表另一欄是母親不工作時的貧窮勝算比：法國 7.6、德國 2.3、義大利 4.6、西班牙 3.3、丹麥 7.8、瑞典 117.6、英國 6.6、美國 4.6。瑞典的極端值是因為幾乎沒有人不工作，少數不工作者很不具代表性（註 28）。

單親家戶（幾乎都是單親母親）占有子女家庭的 15–20% (p. 36)。

p. 37 的對比最有力：瑞典、法國、德國在移轉前的貧窮驚人地接近（50–55%），移轉後卻大幅分歧（分別為 5%、21%、38%）。

作者的判斷 (p. 37)：工作策略有效，但對單親母親只有條件地有效。她們較可能是部分工時或不穩定就業，薪資低。任何實際的方案都要結合補貼（更實際地說免費）的托育，以及不論有沒有工作的所得保障。

講解

p. 37：There is, for example, a surprising degree of convergence in pre-transfer poverty between Sweden, France, and Germany (at 50–55 per cent), but massive divergence in post-transfer poverty (5, 21, and 38 per cent, respectively).

福利陷阱 (p. 39)：Gustafsson et al. (2000) 比較歐洲八個城市，西班牙、葡萄牙、義大利單親母親的救助期間比瑞典長得多，而這三國的給付非常微薄。一個解釋是瑞典轉去工作的機會成本低得多，因為托育免費、薪資較好。原文：The welfare trap may lie buried in day care and employment opportunities rather than in the protestant ethic.

現金保證的兩個理由 (p. 39)：依賴缺席父親的扶養費不切實際；單親母親的工作保障可能非常脆弱。Hence, an exaggerated bias in favour of 'make-work-pay' policy has substantial risks.

給付水準 (p. 38)：丹麥與瑞典的兒童給付，對有兩名幼兒的母親只相當於平均工資的 20%（約每月 340 歐元）。比利時與丹麥的兒童津貼加社會救助，兩名子女的單親家長可達 1,200 歐元與 1,639 歐元；西班牙與義大利幾乎不到 200 歐元。

講解

過度代表的屬性 (p. 40)，歐盟層次：教育極低（45% 低於中學）、單身未婚（55%）、從未就業的女性（女性中 49%）、失業男性（男性中 50%）、家中有一名成人有慢性障礙或健康不佳（33%）、未在找工作（65%）。作者提醒，這些比例重疊，不能相加（註 33）。

四群人見 p. 41。完全不活動的人約占三分之二，失業而在找工作的不到 30%。

p. 43 原文：If we adopt the broadest possible definition and view it as persons who more or less certainly will not respond to activation or employment incentives, the hard-core might include, say, 50–60 per cent of the total ... This leaves us with about 40 per cent of the workless households that can realistically be 'integrated'.

這個估計的意義：若半數以上的無工作家戶不會回應活化，所得維持就是唯一人道又有成本效益的選項 (p. 43)。長期的出路是預防，而預防要從童年開始 (p. 45)。

講解

p. 47：Overall, there is little scientific evidence that social benefits reduce work incentives per se.
