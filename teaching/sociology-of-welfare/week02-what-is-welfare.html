Sociology and social policy

A social policy is an action of a government that has a direct impact on the welfare of its citizens, but might also be about social control.

Most sociologists would agree that sociology should have an improving society. However, the extent to which governments base their policies on social research is not as simple as it might seem.

There are various factors that may influence policymaking and the extent to which sociology is a part of this process.

The “founding fathers”

1798–1857

August Comte

Saw sociology as a practical subject, that shouldn’t remain in universities but should be applied to wider society. The purpose of sociology is ”to know, in order to predict and control society”.

1858–1917

Emile Durkheim

Saw sociology as proving ways of restoring order and strengthening the integration of society.

1818–1883

Karl Marx

Hoped his work would inspire and direct working-class movements in capitalist societies.

Notice how all of these sociologists, and by extension their ideas, are 100 years outdated.

Giddens: the influence of sociology on social policy

  • Sociology can inform policymakers of viewpoints other than their own.
  • Sociological research can also assess the results of policy initiatives, and provide feedback to agencies.
  • Sociology can also generate self-understanding.
  • This knowledge may lead to the questioning of government policies and the creation of protest groups with alternative non-government initiatives.
E.G. ・ AMNESTY INTERNATIONAL

Amnesty International aims to bring about reform of existing policy:

“To conduct research and generate action to prevent and end grave abuses of human rights and to demand justice for those whose rights have been violated”

— Amnesty International Mission Statement

There are many factors that may affect whether or not sociological research influences social policy.

  • Electoral Popularity. Findings and recommendations might point to a policy that would be unpopular with voters.
  • Ideologies and policy preferences of governments.
  • Interest groups; government lobbying.
  • Globalization. Social policy isn't just made by nation states independently.
  • Critical Sociology. Sociologists who are critical of the state and powerful agencies, such as marxists, may be regarded as too extreme, hostile or impractical and therefore unlikely to influence policy.

Sociological research can influence public opinion, which can affect the policies that governments produce. Also, those with power are able to define what is and what is not a social problem, thus can decide what (if anything) should be done about it.

Sociological Problem?

社會學研究什麼？

Any pattern of relationships that calls for an explanation.

Social Problem?

社會問題是什麼？

Social behavior that causes public friction or misery, calling for action to solve it.

社會問題

  • 社會問題是來自於社會運作本身
  • 社會問題不是壞人所引起的
  • 問題是被社會大多數的人定義為會有負面影響的情況、同時需要改變
  • 人們看待問題的方式是不一樣的
  • 對於問題的定義會隨著時間不同
  • 問題涉及到主觀的價值和客觀的事實
  • 很多社會問題可以被解決，但是不是所有社會問題
  • 不同的社會問題是互相有關關聯的

PART 01

福利(welfare)？
福祉(well-being)？

“Well-being”

DEFINITION

“Well-being” is a positive physical, social and mental state; it is not just the absence of pain, discomfort and incapacity, it requires that basic needs are met, that individuals have a sense of purpose, that they feel able to achieve important personal goals and participate in society.

Welfare ・ Daly (2011)

QUOTE ・ Daly 2011

Welfare：不只涉及到我們如何生活於這個社會中，而且涉及到我們如何思考我們生活應該要變成甚麼樣子 (Daly, 2011)

因此，福利社會學可以讓我們了解福利相關的政策工具以及制度，是如何鑲嵌於國家的體系之中，特別是社會政策與方法，以及社會與個人福祉的資源與程度是如何在現實生活中所實現。(Daly, 2011)

因此，福利可以被理解為一套理念和實際的作為，而此貫穿與影響人類在家庭、社區、市場和國家的價值判斷與行為模式(Daly, 2011)。

Welfare

DEFINITION

Welfare: the highest possible access to economic resources, a high level of wellbeing, including the happiness of the citizens, a guaranteed minimum income to avoid living in poverty, and, finally, having the capabilities to ensure the individual a good life.

為什麼需要社會政策？

一種社會契約 (social contract)

社會契約

社會結構由政治和法律等制度、經濟、家庭和社區生活的組方式決定。

決定集體至如何運作的規範和規則，就是社會契約。

  • 期望
  • 相互關係 (mutuality)

我們對彼此有何責任？

Thomas Hobbes

自利但理性的個人應當服從一個絕對主權的權力，這是避免落入殘酷的自然狀態的唯一可靠方法。

John Locke

社會契約的目的是保護公民的生命、自由和福祉；因此主權者未來保護這些權利，公民造反並建立新的政治社會就是正當的。

Jean Jacques Rousseau

關注如何維護自由，但他認識到由於人類越來越互相依賴，為了在良好的社會裡共同生活，妥協是必要的。因此，必須要有政治制度的支持，以便公民制定他們願意服從的法律，從而賦予國家權力正當的基礎。

社會契約只是生活在一個免於剝削的社會的前提條件。

我們對彼此有何責任？

社會契約的轉移到公民責任和我們對彼此的責任

Adam Smith

同理心所促進的社會團結有道德、政治和經濟方面的理由。道德方面，在每一個社會中，個人都有一些基本需求，不滿足這些需求在道德上就是錯誤的。政治上，是為了讓民主制度有效運作。經濟上，安排大量公民共同分擔疾病、失業和養老等風險，比任由個人努力自保更有效率。

John Rawls

無知之幕後面設計的社會契約。機會均等原則和差異原則。

社會契約

是指個人、企業、公民社會與國家共同合作，為一個攸關集體利益的體系做出貢獻。

而社會政策即是靠政治過程和隨後的國家行動居中協調，致力分攤風險和投資於社會利益的機制。

社會福利(Social Welfare)

DEFINITION ・ Barker 1999

「一種國家的方案、給付、服務體系，用來協助人民滿足其社會、經濟、教育與健康需求，此乃社會維持的基礎。」

(Barker 1999)。
