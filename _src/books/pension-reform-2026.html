⟦zh⟧緒論⟦en⟧Foreword⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧勞保基金最快將在 2028 年耗盡。⟦en⟧Taiwan's Labour Insurance fund will run out by 2028.⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧這不僅是財務黑洞，更是台灣世代契約的斷裂——上一代的承諾，能否由下一代的工作來兌現？ ⟦en⟧ This is not just a financial black hole — it is a rupture in the country's intergenerational contract. Can the promises of one generation still be honoured by the labour of the next? ⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧ 過去三十年，台灣的年金改革常常陷入「財務 vs. 福利」的二元辯證，但這樣的框架已經無法回應年金體系正在面對的多重壓力：人口結構、勞動市場彈性化、女性與非典型工作者的給付風險、以及年金資金被導入金融市場後的新治理問題。 ⟦en⟧ For three decades, Taiwan's pension debate has been trapped in a "fiscal vs. welfare" binary. That framing can no longer answer the multiple pressures the system now faces: demographic change, labour-market flexibilisation, new payout risks for women and non-standard workers, and the new governance problems that arise once pension assets are channelled into financial markets. ⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧ 本書集合台灣社會政策、社會工作、經濟學、政治學領域的研究者，從⟦en⟧ This volume gathers researchers across social policy, social work, economics and political science to revisit Taiwan's pension predicament along four axes: ⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧世代正義、性別平等、勞動市場變遷、年金金融化⟦en⟧generational justice, gender equality, labour-market change, and pension financialisation⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧四條軸線，重新檢視台灣年金體系的結構性困境，並納入歐洲與日本近二十年來的改革經驗，提出可行的台灣解方。 ⟦en⟧. Drawing on two decades of European and Japanese reform experience, it sketches workable Taiwanese solutions. ⟦/⟧
