⟦zh⟧ 本書以⟦en⟧ This book takes ⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧台灣、日本、新加坡、韓國、香港⟦en⟧Taiwan, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, and Hong Kong⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧五個東亞社會為案例，深入研究這些社會在政治經濟結構轉型中，其福利體制改革為何呈現截然不同的轉型過程與改革方向。 ⟦en⟧ as comparative cases to investigate why these East Asian societies have followed strikingly different welfare-regime reform pathways during their political-economic transitions. ⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧ 過去的「東亞福利體制」研究常以「儒家文化」、「發展型國家」、「生產主義」等單一概念來涵蓋整個區域，但這樣的框架忽略了東亞各國在⟦en⟧ Earlier "East Asian welfare regime" scholarship has often relied on single-frame concepts — Confucian culture, the developmental state, productivism — to cover the entire region. Such framings obscure the substantive differences in ⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧資本主義型態、政治體制、勞資結構⟦en⟧capitalist varieties, political systems, and labour-capital structures⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧上的實質差異。本書採取比較歷史制度分析的取徑，回到每個案例的具體歷史脈絡，重新檢視為何制度路徑會走向分歧。 ⟦en⟧ that distinguish these societies from one another. This book takes a comparative historical-institutional approach, returning to each case's specific historical context to reconsider why institutional paths diverge. ⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧ 透過跨地域比較，本書反省既有「東亞福利體制」觀點的不足，並提出更細緻的⟦en⟧ Through cross-territorial comparison, the book interrogates the limits of the standard "East Asian welfare regime" perspective and proposes a more granular ⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧多樣性命題⟦en⟧diversity thesis⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧——東亞並非一個體制，而是多種發展路徑的集合。 ⟦en⟧: East Asia is not one regime, but a constellation of developmental pathways. ⟦/⟧

Author ・ 作者

About the Author

Yeh, Chung-Yang
Professor, Soochow University
Comparative welfare states, East-Asian social policy, pension politics, welfare attitudes. Research focuses on pension and social-policy reform in Taiwan, Japan, and Korea, and on the diversity within East Asian welfare regimes. Doctoral work addressed labour-market exit institutions in Germany; subsequent work has examined how East Asian states are reshaping their social contracts under globalisation and post-industrial pressure. Co-editor of The Buried Welfare Pension: Challenges and Solutions of Pension Reform in Taiwan (2026). ⟦/⟧

比較福利國家、東亞社會政策、年金政治、福利態度。研究關注台灣、日本、韓國的年金與社會政策改革，以及東亞福利體制的多樣性。 博士論文以德國勞動市場退出制度為題，後續研究聚焦於東亞國家如何在全球化與後工業化壓力下，重塑各自的社會契約。 合編《葬送的福利年（金）：台灣年金改革的挑戰與解答》（2026）。
