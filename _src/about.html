⟦zh⟧簡介⟦en⟧⟦/⟧About⟦zh⟧⟦en⟧⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧在制度與社會之間寫字，一個福利國家的研究者。⟦en⟧Writing between institutions and society — a researcher of the welfare state.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧福利國家研究者，從英國念博士回來，把比較福利體制的視野放進台灣與東亞的脈絡。長年關注年金、社會投資、福利態度與不平等。⟦en⟧A welfare-state researcher. After completing my PhD in the United Kingdom, I brought a comparative welfare-regime lens back to Taiwan and East Asia. My long-standing concerns are pensions, social investment, welfare attitudes, and inequality.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧研究在比較與制度分析之間移動。希望文字硬一點、視野軟一點——資料是可驗證的，但社會永遠比模型多一層。⟦en⟧My work moves between comparative and institutional analysis. I want the prose to be hard and the vision soft — data should be verifiable, yet society always holds one more layer than the model.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧近年的興趣是福利市場、福利金融化，以及監管型福利國家的崛起。同時也在思考：福利政策怎麼回應一個不斷縮小、變老、變不平等的台灣。⟦en⟧Recent interests run to welfare markets, the financialisation of welfare, and the rise of the regulatory welfare state. I am also asking how social policy should answer to a Taiwan that keeps shrinking, ageing, and growing more unequal.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧攝影⟦en⟧Photography⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧也是攝影師。近三十年，攝影是與研究並行的另一種觀看。風景和街拍都拍，數位和底片都拍。⟦en⟧Also a photographer. For nearly thirty years, photography has been another way of seeing that runs alongside the research. I photograph both landscapes and streets, in digital and on film.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧風景想拍的，是有感情、有個人情緒的風景寫真，不是漂亮的風景照：把看到的東西，透過自己的情緒表達出來。喜歡藍調時刻，因為這個時間拍出來的東西不一樣。也喜歡印象派和透納，透過顏色之間的構圖來表達情緒，同時反映光線。⟦en⟧In landscape, I look for a photograph with feeling and personal emotion rather than a pretty view: to express what I saw through my own emotion. I am fond of the blue hour, because what is photographed at that time is different. I also love the Impressionists and Turner, who express emotion through the composition of colours while reflecting the light.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧街拍更負有社會責任。它不只是個人情緒的出口，也是拍攝者對既有社會結構的批判。⟦en⟧Street photography carries more social responsibility. It is not only an outlet for personal emotion; it is also the photographer’s critique of existing social structures.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧偏好日本的攝影風格。喜歡的攝影師有植田正治、前田真三、中西敏貴，以及 Josef Koudelka 和 André Kertész。⟦en⟧My taste leans towards Japanese photography. The photographers I admire include Shoji Ueda, Shinzo Maeda and Toshiki Nakanishi, as well as Josef Koudelka and André Kertész.⟦/⟧
