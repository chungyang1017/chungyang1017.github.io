⟦zh⟧一⟦en⟧1⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧福利國家研究者，從英國念博士回來，把比較福利體制的視野放進台灣與東亞的脈絡。長年關注年金、社會投資、福利態度與不平等。⟦en⟧A welfare-state researcher. After completing my PhD in the United Kingdom, I brought a comparative welfare-regime lens back to Taiwan and East Asia. My long-standing concerns are pensions, social investment, welfare attitudes, and inequality.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧二⟦en⟧2⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧研究在比較與制度分析之間移動。希望文字硬一點、視野軟一點——資料是可驗證的，但社會永遠比模型多一層。⟦en⟧My work moves between comparative and institutional analysis. I want the prose to be hard and the vision soft — data should be verifiable, yet society always holds one more layer than the model.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧三⟦en⟧3⟦/⟧⟦zh⟧近年的興趣是福利市場、福利金融化，以及監管型福利國家的崛起。同時也在思考：福利政策怎麼回應一個不斷縮小、變老、變不平等的台灣。⟦en⟧Recent interests run to welfare markets, the financialisation of welfare, and the rise of the regulatory welfare state. I am also asking how social policy should answer to a Taiwan that keeps shrinking, ageing, and growing more unequal.⟦/⟧
