⟦zh⟧The Multidimensional Pension Preferences⟦en⟧NSTC Research Project⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧多面向的年金政策偏好：⟦en⟧The Multidimensional Pension Preferences:⟦/⟧
⟦zh⟧資訊、政治交換與東亞社會大眾的年金改革偏好分析⟦en⟧Information, the Politics of Trade-offs, and Pension Preferences in East Asia⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧年金改革是東亞福利國家共同的難題：制度必須回應人口老化與財政壓力，改革卻往往在民意面前卻步。本計畫追問——社會大眾的年金改革偏好從何而來？它如何被資訊改變、被交換（／權衡）政治說服？⟦en⟧Pension reform is a challenge East Asian welfare states share: institutions must respond to population ageing and fiscal pressure, yet reform stalls in the face of public opinion. This project asks — where do public preferences for pension reform come from, how are they moved by information, and how are they won over by the politics of trade-offs?⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧⟦en⟧Three Axes⟦/⟧研究主軸⟦zh⟧Three Axes⟦en⟧⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧多面向的年金偏好⟦en⟧Multidimensional preferences⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧年金改革偏好並非單純的支持或反對。把它拆開成給付水準、財源組合、退休年齡與世代分配的權衡，描繪社會大眾偏好的內在結構。⟦en⟧Preferences for pension reform are not a simple yes or no. Unpacking them into trade-offs over benefit levels, financing mixes, retirement ages and intergenerational allocation maps the inner structure of public preferences.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧資訊⟦en⟧Information⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧民眾對年金制度的理解往往有限。當制度財務、人口趨勢與改革後果的資訊被攤開，偏好會如何移動？檢視資訊在偏好形成中的角色。⟦en⟧Public understanding of pension systems is often limited. When information about system finances, demographic trends and reform consequences is laid out, how do preferences move? This axis examines the role of information in preference formation.⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧交換（／權衡）政治⟦en⟧The politics of trade-offs⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧改革從來不是單一政策的公投，而是配套與補償的交換。哪些交換（／權衡）的組合，能讓社會大眾接受痛苦的改革？⟦en⟧Reform is never a referendum on a single policy; it is an exchange of packages and compensation. Which combinations of trade-offs make painful reform acceptable to the public?⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧⟦en⟧Research Design⟦/⟧研究設計⟦zh⟧Design⟦en⟧⟦/⟧

⟦zh⟧以跨國比較的調查實驗為核心，在東亞社會同步施測——用聯合實驗（conjoint experiment）讓受訪者在完整的改革方案之間做選擇，而不是對單一政策表態。⟦en⟧The project is built around cross-national survey experiments fielded simultaneously across East Asian societies — conjoint experiments ask respondents to choose between complete reform packages rather than react to single policies.⟦/⟧
